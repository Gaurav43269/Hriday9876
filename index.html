A history in six eras

Thirty years of
digital crime.

A small Scottish studio built a top-down game about stealing cars in 1997. What followed was a two-and-a-half-decade rewrite of what an open world could be — and one of the most valuable entertainment franchises ever made.

Grand Theft Auto did not invent crime games, but it invented a template almost every open-world game since has borrowed from: a city that keeps living whether or not you engage with it, and a story told through the freedom to ignore it.

This is the shape of that history — from a bird's-eye car-theft sim made in Dundee to a fictional Florida the whole industry is currently waiting on. Every scene below is an original illustration built for this page, not game footage.

The eras

Six reinventions of the same idea.

1997–99

Top-Down Origins

Built by DMA Design in Dundee, Scotland, the original games were played from directly above — closer to a board game than a movie — and were flagged by parts of the press for rewarding, rather than punishing, lawbreaking.

  • 1997Grand Theft Auto — three fictional cities, missions dialled in from a payphone, and a moral panic that gave the series more publicity than any ad campaign could.
  • 1997GTA: London 1969 — a standalone expansion swapping the setting for Swinging London, right down to left-hand traffic.
  • 1999Grand Theft Auto 2 — added a reputation system tied to rival gangs, in a stylised near-future setting called Anywhere City.

2001–02

The Third Dimension

DMA Design became Rockstar North, and the series jumped into full 3D. This is the two-year stretch most people mean when they talk about GTA "changing everything."

  • 2001Grand Theft Auto III — the first fully 3D open world in the series, a silent protagonist, and a template for an entire genre that followed.
  • 2002Vice City — an '80s Miami pastiche with a voiced lead, a wardrobe, real estate to buy, and a licensed soundtrack spanning nine in-game radio stations.

2004–06

The Golden Age

The series went bigger — a whole fictional state instead of one city — while quietly filling out a handheld side-catalogue most players never touched.

  • 2004San Andreas — three cities across one state, RPG-style stats, gang territory wars, and one of the best-selling console games ever made.
  • 2004GTA Advance — a top-down spin-off built for the Game Boy Advance.
  • 2005–06Liberty City & Vice City Stories — PSP prequels revisiting earlier cities with new protagonists, later ported back to PS2.
One state, three citiesRPG statsPS2 / PSP
2008–09

The Realism Era

A new engine and a new console generation pushed the series toward weight and consequence — heavier physics, a grimmer story, and a reimagined Liberty City standing in for New York.

  • 2008Grand Theft Auto IV — a new physics and animation engine, and immigrant Niko Bellic's more grounded, satirical take on the American dream.
  • 2009Episodes from Liberty City — two episodic expansions, The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, later packaged as a standalone release.
  • 2009Chinatown Wars — a handheld return to the top-down view, built around touchscreen minigames.
New physics engineLiberty City rebuiltXbox 360 / PS3
2013–

The Modern Era

One game has now spanned three console generations without a numbered sequel — because its online mode turned out to be the more valuable product.

  • 2013Grand Theft Auto V — three interlocking protagonists across a fictional Los Santos and Blaine County, standing in for Southern California.
  • 2013GTA Online — a persistent multiplayer mode that grew, through a decade of continuous updates, into the series' primary source of revenue.
  • 2014–22Three re-releases — the same base game rebuilt for PS4/Xbox One, PC, and finally PS5/Xbox Series in an "Expanded and Enhanced" edition.

The next chapter

Grand Theft Auto VI

Announced in December 2023 with a trailer that broke online video-viewing records within its first day, GTA VI moves the series to the fictional state of Leonida — including a reimagined, present-day Vice City — told through two playable leads, Jason and Lucia.

Originally aimed at a 2025 window, the release has since shifted twice, with Rockstar most recently setting the date for November 19, 2026, citing the extra time needed to finish the game at the standard the series is known for.
